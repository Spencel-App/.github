# Security Policy

Spencel handles people's financial information, so we take security seriously and we're
grateful to researchers who help us keep it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities **privately**. Do **not** open a public issue,
pull request, or discussion for a security problem, and please don't disclose it publicly
until we've had a chance to investigate and ship a fix.

### What to include

To help us triage quickly, please include where you can:

- A clear description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected repository, component, or endpoint, and any relevant version or commit.
- Any suggested remediation, if you have one.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement:** we aim to confirm we've received your report within a few business
  days.
- **Updates:** we'll keep you informed as we investigate and work on a fix.
- **Resolution:** once a fix is released, we're happy to credit you for the discovery if
  you'd like.

## Scope

This policy covers the repositories in the Spencel organization. Please only test against
your own accounts and data. Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data that isn't yours,
and avoid actions that could degrade the service or affect other users (for example,
denial-of-service testing or automated scanning at scale).

## Safe harbor

We won't pursue or support legal action against researchers who act in good faith, follow
this policy, and give us a reasonable chance to address an issue before any public
disclosure. Thank you for helping keep Spencel and its users safe.
